You have zero live chat or chatbot channels configured. All 912 conversations in the last 6 months came through email (98%) or forms (2%). With 6,867 tickets, a chatbot could deflect common questions and route complex ones faster. Service Enterprise includes advanced bot builders with branching logic.

### 2. Knowledge Base (Service Enterprise)

No knowledge base detected. This is one of the highest-ROI Service Enterprise features — self-service articles that deflect tickets before they're created. With your ticket volume, even a small KB covering top 10 issues could meaningfully reduce support load.

### 3. Blog & Content (CMS)

Zero blog posts. Zero landing pages. You have CMS tools available but no inbound content. Your marketing team (6 people) is sending emails but has no content engine driving organic traffic or lead capture beyond forms.

### 4. Landing Pages

Zero landing pages. Your 126 marketing emails are sending people somewhere, but not to HubSpot-tracked landing pages. That means you're missing conversion attribution — you can't see which email drove which form fill.

### 5. Customer Feedback Surveys (Service Enterprise)

No feedback surveys found. NPS, CSAT, and CES surveys are included with Service Enterprise. With nearly 7,000 tickets, you have no systematic way to measure customer satisfaction or identify service trends.

### 6. Marketing Events

Zero marketing events tracked. If Forgent attends trade shows, hosts webinars, or runs any events, you're not tracking attendee engagement or attributing pipeline to those efforts.

### 7. Commerce / Invoicing (Commerce Enterprise)

You have Commerce Enterprise seats but zero invoices. Payments and payment links also appear inactive. If you're invoicing outside HubSpot, you're missing the closed-loop between deals, quotes, and revenue collection.

### 8. Campaign Attribution (underused)

Only 7 campaigns across 126 marketing emails. Most emails aren't organized into campaigns, which means you can't do multi-touch attribution or measure which marketing efforts actually influenced pipeline. Your Sales Ops team is flying partially blind on marketing ROI.

## Highest-impact recommendations (in priority order)

1. **Stand up a Knowledge Base** — lowest effort, highest ticket deflection. Start with your top 10 ticket categories. Service Enterprise includes this.

2. **Deploy a chatbot on your website** — route RFQ submissions, warranty claims, and support requests. You already have forms for these; a bot adds real-time triage without adding headcount.

3. **Organize emails into campaigns** — you're sending 5,000+ emails/month but can't attribute pipeline to marketing. Start tagging every email to a campaign so Sales Ops can see marketing ROI.

4. **Build landing pages for email CTAs** — close the attribution loop. Every email should point to a tracked landing page with a form, not an untracked external page.

5. **Launch CSAT surveys on ticket close** — with Service Enterprise you can auto-send a satisfaction survey when a ticket is resolved. Instant quality signal for your support operation.

6. **Audit your Commerce Enterprise seats** — if you're not using invoicing or payments, you may be paying for seats you don't need, or there's a workflow integration opportunity with your Finance team.

7. **Clean your email lists** — the July bounce spike needs a root cause. Scrub contacts that haven't engaged in 6+ months to protect your sender reputation.
